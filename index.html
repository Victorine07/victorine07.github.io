About

I'm a computer scientist (Ph.D., 2026) working at the intersection of machine learning and security. My research builds provable, verifiable guarantees for AI-enabled systems, and I'm now focused on empirical AI safety — deception detection, model integrity, and oversight by verification.

My dissertation, From Perceived Trust to Provable Security, is the throughline of my work: don't trust behavior — verify it.

Research Interests

AI Safety & Deception Detection

Probing and interpretability for scheming/deception; scalable oversight; robust detection that survives persona & formatting confounds.

Trustworthy & Verified AI
